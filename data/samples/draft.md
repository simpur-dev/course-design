# Related Work (probe draft)

The Transformer relies entirely on attention mechanisms and dispenses with recurrence and convolutions [@vaswani2017].

BERT uses a bidirectional Transformer encoder and is pre-trained with masked language modeling and next sentence prediction [@devlin2019].

The Transformer was pre-trained on 3.3 billion tokens from BooksCorpus and English Wikipedia [@vaswani2017].

BERT's largest model has 340 million parameters [@devlin2019].
